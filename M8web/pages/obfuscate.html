Image Obfuscation

Three modes: Tomato cycles pixels along a curve, scramble shuffles in keyed blocks, and nest hides another image in the low bits. All three are reversible and computed on this machine; the image is never uploaded.

Always save an obfuscated image as PNG. Do not crop or resize it either - all three modes need the original dimensions to reverse. Scramble is a pure pixel permutation, and nest puts its payload in the least significant bit of every pixel: once the file becomes a JPEG, those low bits are thrown away and nobody can undo it.

With no key filled in, anyone who has the image can reverse it.

Tomato takes no key and has no other parameters - the shift amount is fixed. This is carried over from the original algorithm on purpose: an image obfuscated there can be reversed here, and one obfuscated here can be reversed there.
